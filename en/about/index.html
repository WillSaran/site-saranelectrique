About Saran Électrique

A young team of master electricians from Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu, for your residential and commercial projects.

Who we are

Saran Électrique is a young electrical contractor from Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu. Our dynamic team handles your residential and commercial work, new construction and renovation alike: EV chargers, electrical services, lighting, backup batteries and more.

We show up with well-equipped trucks, work cleanly and take the time to answer your questions. That’s what our customers mention most often in their reviews.
