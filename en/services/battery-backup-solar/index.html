How it works during an outage

1

Before the outage

The battery charges from the Hydro grid, or from your solar panels if you have them. When a storm is forecast, the system can top it up in advance.

2

During the outage

The backup switch isolates your home from the grid and the battery takes over automatically, with nothing for you to do. It powers the circuits we chose together.

3

To last longer

Add a source to recharge the battery: a generator, solar panels or your compatible electric vehicle. The system can also shed less important loads as the battery runs down.

How long does it last? It depends on the installed capacity and what you want to keep running. We do the math with you before any quote. No runtime promises without an assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need solar panels?

No. The battery can be installed on its own: it charges from the Hydro grid and takes over during an outage. Solar can be added whenever you want, to recharge the battery during the day and lower your consumption.

How long will the battery last during an outage?

It depends on the installed capacity and what you want to keep running. Without solar or a generator, it lasts as long as its stored kWh allow. We do the math with you before giving you a quote: we don’t promise a runtime without an assessment.

Can it power the whole house?

Sometimes, yes. It depends on the system’s power and the appliances to run. Many projects cover only essential loads: furnace, fridge, sump pump, internet and lighting.

What if I already have a generator?

It can be paired with the system to last longer during a long outage, depending on the model and setup. We confirm it together when preparing your quote.

Can my electric vehicle act as a battery?

With Sigenergy’s bidirectional DC charging module, a compatible vehicle can power your home during an outage (V2H). Compatibility depends on the vehicle: we check it with you.

What’s the difference between kW and kWh?

kW is the power available at a given moment (how many appliances at once). kWh is the energy stored or used over time (how long it lasts).

Can the system be expanded later?

Yes. Battery modules stack: you can start small and add capacity, solar panels or a charger later, depending on the available space.

Can I monitor my system?

Yes. The mySigen app shows production, consumption, battery status and vehicle charging in real time.
