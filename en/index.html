Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to install an EV charger?

It mainly depends on your panel’s capacity and the distance between the panel and the charger. Start your quote online: place the charger on your house, add a photo of your panel, and we’ll get back to you with an accurate price.

Is my electrical panel big enough?

Many homes have a 100 A panel. With an EV charger, heat pump or hot tub, a 200 A upgrade is sometimes needed. A photo of your panel with the door open often lets us answer without a visit.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Saran Électrique is a member of the Corporation des maîtres électriciens du Québec (CMEQ) and holds RBQ licence 5819-9274-01.

What areas do you serve?

Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu and the whole South Shore: Chambly, La Prairie, Brossard, Saint-Lambert, Marieville, Farnham and more.
